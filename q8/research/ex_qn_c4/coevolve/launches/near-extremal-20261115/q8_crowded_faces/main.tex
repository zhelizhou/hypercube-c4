\section{C01: Local quantities and precise inputs}
In any C4-free $H\subseteq Q_6$, fix either full parity$P$ and put
$L=\#\{v\in P:d(v)\le2\}$,
$S=\sum_{v\in P}\binom{d(v)}3$, and
$c=\#\{v\in P:d(v)=6\}$. The opposite parity is unrestricted.
There are160 coordinate $Q_3$ faces; no such face has two full
specified three-stars, since these would produce a square. Thus
$S\le160$. Also$c\le4$: distinct full roots have distance at least
four, whereas five such roots would have distance sum at least40
and at most $6\cdot6=36$. Finally
\[
                 S\equiv32-L+c\pmod3.                  \tag{1}
\]
This follows by listing degrees0 through6: the low degrees contribute
zero, degrees3,4,5 contribute1 modulo3 and degree6 contributes2.

We retain the actual historical local imports $2S+c\le316,317,321$
for $L=0,1,2$, respectively. Their precise proofs and low-degree safe
replacement interfaces are those in q8\_single\_three\_cycle007/
GENERAL\_WEIGHTED\_GATE.md and local\_weighted/PROOF.md,
TWO\_LOW\_EXTENSION.md. Historical finite certificate replays are not
repeated here. Two newer analytic imports have no degree premise on
either parity: exactly three full roots imply $S+L\le158$
(q6\_three\_full\_structure\_cycle010/PROOF.md); exactly four imply
$S+L\le144$ (q8\_triple\_geometry\_cycle010/PROOF.md).

\section{C02: Sharper local caps for every low-root count}
Define
\[
 t_*(L)=\begin{cases}
 0&L=0,\\1&L=1,\\5&L=2,\\
 6&L\ge3,\ L\equiv0\pmod3,\\
 4&L\ge3,\ L\equiv1\pmod3,\\
 5&L\ge3,\ L\equiv2\pmod3.
 \end{cases}
\]
\begin{theorem}
Every such actual $Q_6$ obeys $2S+c\le316+t_*(L)$.
\end{theorem}
For $L\le2$ this is exactly the declared import. For $L\ge3$, the
cases$c=3,4$ have respective upper bounds $319-2L$ and $292-2L$,
both at most313 and hence below all the asserted caps. For$c=0,1,2$,
apply $S\le160$ and (1). The respective upper bounds for$2S+c$,
listed in that order, are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
L\bmod3&c=0&c=1&c=2&\text{maximum}\\\hline
0&316&319&322&322\\
1&320&317&320&320\\
2&318&321&318&321.
\end{array}
\]
These exhaust$c=0,1,2,3,4$ and prove the claim. No sharpness or
existence of a graph attaining a row is asserted.

\section{C03: Lift back to every683-edge original graph}
Let $G\subseteq Q_8$ be any C4-free graph with683 edges and choose
either specified global parity$P$. Let$n_d$ count its actual degree$d$
vertices. In each of the same112 actual $Q_6$ faces form$L_F,S_F,c_F$.
Put
\[
\begin{split}
 C(P)&=735n_0+534n_1+333n_2+177n_3+66n_4+105n_7+345n_8,\\
 \Phi_*&=\sum_F\left(\frac52 L_F-t_*(L_F)\right),\\
 \delta_F^*&=316+t_*(L_F)-(2S_F+c_F).
\end{split}
\]
Then the exact same-object lift gives
\[
             C(P)+\Phi_*+\sum_F\delta_F^*=1149,          \tag{2}
\]
so $C(P)+\Phi_*\le1149$. For completeness, each global specified
three-star belongs to10 such faces, a degree$d$ root is full in
$\binom d6$ of them, and is low in respectively
$(28,28,28,18,6,0,0,0,0)_d$ of them. Substitute these counts into
the sum of the local definitions, then eliminate$n_5,n_6$ using
$\sum n_d=128$ and $\sum dn_d=683$; (2) results.

Every summand in$\Phi_*$ is nonnegative and is zero exactly when
$L_F=0,2$. Compared with the previous loss
$\Phi=\sum_F(5L_F/2-t(L_F))$, where
$t(0,1,2,3)=(0,1,5,6)$ and $t(L)=8$ for$L\ge4$, the improvement
is exactly
\[
 \Phi_*-\Phi=\sum_{F:L_F\ge4}(8-t_*(L_F)).              \tag{3}
\]
For counts congruent to1,2,0 modulo3 these additional losses are4,3,2,
respectively. All faces are restrictions of the same original graph;
no independently feasible local completions are substituted.

The equality case $C(P)=1149$ has the same0/2 condition as before;
this does not remove either remaining zero-slack degree profile.
No new numerical unrestricted bound or complete profile exclusion
is asserted. The original upper682 remains open.
