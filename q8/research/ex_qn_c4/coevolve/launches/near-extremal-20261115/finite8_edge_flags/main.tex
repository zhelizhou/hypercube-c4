\paragraph{Result and scope.}
Edge-root PSD blocks are pointwise contained in the original full-face
PSD family.  Integer-seven frequency kernels can nevertheless give
additional directions within the larger full-face finite-six cone.
This package gives three exact such kernels and their actual negative
target pairings.  The original full upper bound682 remains open.
The target of the third kernel is a frozen numerical intermediate,
not a claimed feasible graph or relaxation point.  All previous source,
point and review files are read only.

\section{E01: actual states and the exact edge bridge}

Orient an ambient root edge from \(u\) to \(v\), with \(u<v\).
Let \(r\in\{0,1\}\) be its retained value.  For an external direction,
its full \(Q_2\) flag records three bits: the connector at \(u\), the
connector at \(v\), then the edge opposite \(uv\).
For \(r=0\), states \(0,\ldots,7\) are legal.  For \(r=1\), state7
would close a \(C_4\), so states \(0,\ldots,6\) are legal.
The common endpoint flip is
\[
 f(s)=(s\mathbin{\&}4)+2(s\mathbin{\&}1)
                  +((s\mathbin{\&}2)/2).
\]
For a symmetric kernel \(Q_r\), define
\(\overline Q_r=(Q_r+P_fQ_rP_f^{\mathsf T})/2\).
Both flag arguments use the same endpoint action.

For a legal labelled \(K\subseteq Q_4\), each of its32 ambient edges
has three external flags \(s_1,s_2,s_3\).  Define
\[
 T_{\rm edge}(K)=\frac1{672}\sum_{e\subset Q_4}
 \left(6\sum_{a<b}\overline Q_{r_e}[s_a,s_b]
              +\sum_a\overline Q_{r_e}[s_a,s_a]\right).
 \tag{E01.1}
\]
All entries come from the same actual \(K\).

\begin{theorem}[Exact whole-\(Q_8\) bridge]\label{thm:edge}
For any actual \(C_4\)-free \(G\subseteq Q_8\), if \(x_e\) is the count
vector of its seven external flags at ambient edge \(e\), then
\[
 \E_{K\text{ uniform coordinate }Q_4}T_{\rm edge}(K)
 =\frac1{1024}\sum_{e\subset Q_8}
       \frac{x_e^{\mathsf T}\overline Q_{r_e}x_e}{49}.
 \tag{E01.2}
\]
Thus the expectation is nonnegative for PSD kernels, or for any kernels
nonnegative on all nonnegative integer total-seven vectors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each global edge is in \(\binom73=35\) of the1120 coordinate \(Q_4\)'s.
Uniform restriction/root sampling therefore makes the1024 global edges
uniform and selects a uniform three-subset of their seven external axes.
For \(u=x_e/7\), independent draws give
\[
 uu^{\mathsf T}=\frac67R_{\ne}+\frac17\operatorname{diag}(u).
\]
The mean over the three local unordered cross pairs is the distinct-axis
average for a symmetric kernel; the mean over the three diagonal terms
is the single-axis average.  Formula (E01.1) is exactly
\(1/32\) times \(6/7\) of the former plus \(1/7\) of the latter.
This proves the identity.  Its final implication follows term by term,
since every \(x_e\) is a nonnegative integer vector of total seven.
No degree, parity or seed condition is used.
\end{proof}

With denominator64512, each endpoint action contributes288 to a cross
entry and48 to a diagonal entry in the symmetric upper-coordinate
linear score.  Equivalently the pre-average coefficients are
\(96(6\,\text{cross}+\text{diagonal})\).
\file{edge_flags.py} reconstructs these coefficients in the original
bottom12/top12/cross8 \(Q_4\) edge-mask order.

\section{E02: exact pullback and PSD redundancy}

Fix a root face \(F\) and one of its four edges \(e\).
Let \(s_0\) be \(F\) viewed as a one-direction edge flag at \(e\).
Each external full-face flag \(g\) determines the edge flag \(s(g)\)
at that same edge.  In the appropriate edge state space set
\[
 v_g=6e_{s(g)}+e_{s_0},\qquad
 V_{F,e}=(v_g)_g,\qquad
 P_F=\frac1{196}\sum_{e\subset F}V_{F,e}^{\mathsf T}
                      \overline Q_{r_e}V_{F,e}.
 \tag{E02.1}
\]
All vectors use actual bits and common endpoint frames.

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise containment and integer pullback]\label{thm:lift}
Using \(P_F\) in the original finite-eight face feature gives exactly
\(T_{\rm edge}(K)\) on every legal labelled \(K\subseteq Q_4\).
If the edge kernels are PSD, all five full-face kernels are PSD.
If they are only valid on every integer total-seven histogram, all
five full-face kernels are valid on every integer total-six histogram.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Endpoint averaging makes (E02.1) invariant under each actual face
stabilizer: a face symmetry permutes its four edges and may flip their
endpoints, always acting on all associated flags together.

Fix a local root edge and its three external flags \(s_0,s_a,s_b\).
The finite-eight face law is \(5/6\) cross plus \(1/12\) per diagonal.
For the face having base \(s_0\), expansion of (E02.1) gives, before
\(1/(24\cdot196)\),
\[
 30Q(s_a,s_b)+6Q(s_0,s_a)+6Q(s_0,s_b)
 +3Q(s_a,s_a)+3Q(s_b,s_b)+Q(s_0,s_0).
\]
Summing the three base choices gives coefficient \(30+6+6=42\)
on each unordered cross and \(1+3+3=7\) on each diagonal.
Since \(7/(24\cdot196)=1/672\), this is (E01.1) exactly.
This proves pointwise equality, not merely equality of global averages.

For PSD edge kernels, congruence and summation in (E02.1) preserve PSD.
For the second claim take any full-face integer count vector \(x\ge0\)
of total six.  For each edge,
\[
 V_{F,e}x=6z_e,\qquad
 z_e=\sum_gx_ge_{s(g)}+e_{s_0},\qquad
 z_e\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^{d_{r_e}},\quad\sum z_e=7.
\]
Legality of the full-face state ensures every read edge-flag state is
in its correct \(7\)- or \(8\)-state space.
Therefore \(x^{\mathsf T}P_Fx=(36/196)\sum_e
z_e^{\mathsf T}\overline Q_{r_e}z_e\ge0\).
\end{proof}

Thus edge PSD supplies no new relaxation strength.  Finite-seven edge
directions are contained in the complete finite-six face-kernel cone,
but can be useful beyond a currently selected finite list of cuts.
The archive's old \file{fold_old.py} already absorbed earlier partial-edge
PSD blocks; this package does not claim a newly discovered PSD capability.
Its finite-eight normalization and integer-count pullback are explicit.

\[
 M_r=\frac{U_r}{2\cdot64512W};
\]
an upper off-diagonal coefficient is split between the two symmetric

\section{E05: complete small cone and consumer interface}

The strongest finite caller need not retain only three target kernels.
For each \(r\), the complete necessary moment condition is
\[
 M_r(\lambda)=
 \sum_{x\in\Omega_7(d_r)}\beta_{r,x}
 \frac{xx^{\mathsf T}+P_fx x^{\mathsf T}P_f^{\mathsf T}}{98},
 \qquad \beta_{r,x}\ge0.
 \tag{E05.1}
\]
This preserves the same original local-graph weights \(\lambda\);
\(\beta\) are auxiliary frequency coefficients, not separately selected
graphs.  Each averaged generator has ordered entry sum one, so the sum
of \(\beta_{r,x}\) equals the root-type probability already represented
by \(M_r(\lambda)\).  No separately fixed conditional mass is needed.
The complete domain has only \(3432+1716\) generators.
It is valid for actual graphs by the integer-seven root law and common
endpoint transport.  Satisfying it does not produce a graph.

For a selected cut, \file{score.value_on_graph(g,name)} gives its exact
normalized feature, with positive scales1320,828,1680 in the order above.
\file{score.full_face_lift(name)} returns five existing face-block
integer numerator matrices and denominator \(196\) times the scale.
The native face scorer can therefore evaluate the new direction without
changing its legal graph domain.  Candidate weights for these valid
features must be nonnegative; a PSD-only Gram acceptance wrapper cannot
certify them.

For the actual682 seed all three normalized means are nonnegative.
The snapshot cut's seed mean is \(1/3840\), and its exact snapshot mean is
\[
 -\frac{11552289982752784987417}{11200000000000082142383680}.
\]
Its value on the legal oracle graph1774744440 is \(11/120\).
These are interface regressions, not graph exclusions.
